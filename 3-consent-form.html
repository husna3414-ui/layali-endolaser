What is Endolaser?

Endolaser is a minimally invasive laser treatment used to tighten skin, stimulate collagen production, improve skin laxity, contour the face or body, and reduce small areas of localised fat.

The treatment involves inserting a very fine sterile laser fibre underneath the skin through a small entry point. Laser energy is then delivered beneath the skin to create controlled heating within the tissue.

This controlled thermal effect may help to:

  • Contract existing collagen fibres
  • Stimulate new collagen production
  • Improve skin firmness
  • Improve skin texture
  • Tighten loose skin
  • Improve jawline and neck definition
  • Reduce jowls
  • Reduce double chin fullness
  • Reduce small localised fat deposits
  • Improve contour in selected body areas

Endolaser is not the same as surgery and does not produce the same result as a surgical facelift, neck lift or liposuction.

Procedure

The treatment area will be assessed, marked and cleansed prior to treatment.

A local anaesthetic may be used to improve comfort. A small entry point is created in the skin, and a fine sterile laser fibre is inserted under the skin.

Laser energy is delivered into the tissue to create a controlled heating effect. The practitioner will move the fibre carefully through the treatment area according to the treatment plan.

During the procedure, you may feel:

  • Warmth
  • Pressure
  • Mild pulling
  • Mild discomfort
  • Tightness
  • A strange but tolerable sensation under the skin

The procedure time varies depending on the area being treated.

After treatment, the area may feel swollen, tender, tight or bruised. Entry points may be covered with a small dressing if required.

Intended benefits

Endolaser uses a fine laser fibre placed beneath the skin to heat the tissue in a controlled way. This encourages the existing collagen to contract and stimulates new collagen over the following weeks and months, which is what produces the tightening and contouring effect.

What Endolaser may improve

  • Mild to moderate skin laxity and loose skin
  • Jawline and neck definition
  • Jowls and a soft or full lower face
  • Double chin fullness and small localised fat deposits
  • Skin firmness, texture and quality
  • Contour in selected body areas (e.g. abdomen, arms, inner thighs, flanks)

What Endolaser cannot improve

Endolaser is not surgery and does not give the same result as a surgical facelift, neck lift or liposuction. It cannot lift very heavy or severely loose skin, remove large volumes of fat, correct significant sagging, act as a weight-loss treatment, or stop the natural ageing process.

Level of change, timing and how long it lasts

  • Level of change: the improvement is usually gradual and can range from subtle to moderate; it is not a dramatic overnight change.
  • When results appear: some tightening may be felt soon after treatment, but this is partly swelling. The real result comes from gradual collagen tightening over the following weeks. Collagen remodelling typically continues for around 3–6 months, and some clients keep improving for up to 12 months.
  • When it is assessed: the outcome is reviewed at your follow-up appointment, usually once initial swelling has settled and collagen has had time to build (commonly around 8–12 weeks, with a further review later if needed).
  • How long it lasts: results are not permanent. The natural ageing process continues, so the effect softens over time.
  • Maintenance: more than one session may be advised for the best result, and periodic maintenance treatments may be recommended to preserve the effect. Weight changes, smoking, alcohol, diet, hydration and sun exposure all affect how the result develops and lasts.

I understand that these are potential benefits only, that results develop gradually as collagen tightens over weeks, that results vary between individuals, and that no guarantee has been or can be given regarding my result.

Contraindications & reasons to postpone

Before undergoing Endolaser, it is essential to be aware of contraindications and circumstances that may restrict, postpone or prevent treatment. Endolaser may not be suitable if you have:

  • Pregnancy
  • Breastfeeding
  • Active infection
  • Fever or illness
  • Active skin infection in the treatment area
  • Open wounds
  • Severe acne in the treatment area
  • Active cold sores in the treatment area
  • Uncontrolled diabetes
  • Poor wound healing
  • Keloid scarring history
  • Bleeding disorder
  • Blood thinning medication use
  • Severe autoimmune disease
  • Immunosuppression
  • Recent surgery in the treatment area
  • Recent filler, threads or other aesthetic procedure in the treatment area
  • Unrealistic expectations
  • Significant medical conditions not cleared for treatment
  • Allergy to anaesthetic or products used
  • Active cancer or recent cancer treatment unless medically cleared

Risks and complications

Before undergoing this procedure, it is essential to clearly understand the associated risks.

While every effort is made to minimise risks, no procedure is entirely risk-free. Endolaser is a minimally invasive procedure and involves the insertion of a laser fibre beneath the skin, meaning there are inherent and potential risks.

The following risks may occur, and there may also be unforeseen risks that are not included on this list. Some complications may require medication, medical review, referral, further treatment, corrective procedures or hospital care.

Damage to underlying structures

I understand that Endolaser is performed beneath the skin and, although care is taken, there is a risk of trauma or irritation to underlying structures. These may include:

  • Blood vessels
  • Nerves
  • Lymphatic vessels
  • Fat tissue
  • Connective tissue
  • Salivary glands in facial areas
  • Muscle or fascia
  • Other soft tissue structures

In rare cases, injury may cause persistent problems with appearance, sensation or function.

Infection

I understand that any procedure involving entry through the skin carries a risk of infection. Signs of infection may include increasing redness, increasing swelling, heat, worsening pain, pus or discharge, fever, feeling generally unwell, and red streaking from the area. I understand that if I experience any signs of infection, I must contact Layali Clinic immediately and may need antibiotics or medical review.

Burns and thermal injury

I understand that Endolaser works by delivering heat beneath the skin. Although the treatment is carried out carefully, there is a risk of excessive heat causing burns, blistering, skin damage, skin necrosis, pigmentation change, scarring or delayed healing. I understand that burns and thermal injury may require medical treatment and may leave permanent marks.

Numbness, nerve irritation and altered sensation

I understand that temporary numbness, tingling or altered sensation can occur after Endolaser due to swelling, inflammation or irritation of small sensory nerves. In most cases, this improves with time. However, I understand that in rare cases numbness, altered sensation, hypersensitivity, weakness or nerve symptoms may be prolonged or permanent.

Fibrosis, lumps and unevenness

I understand that during healing I may feel firm areas, lumps, nodules, rope-like firmness, uneven texture or tight bands under the skin. These may be caused by swelling, inflammation, collagen remodelling, fibrosis or tissue healing. Most mild irregularities improve over time, but some may persist and require massage, review, further treatment or corrective management. I understand I must not aggressively massage the area unless specifically advised by my practitioner.

Fat reduction and contour irregularity

Where Endolaser is used for fat reduction, I understand that the reduction may be uneven. Possible contour-related risks include uneven fat reduction, indentations, skin depressions, asymmetry, over-reduction, under-correction, irregular contour, loose skin remaining, and need for additional treatment. I understand that Endolaser is not a weight loss treatment.

Scarring

Although entry points are small, I understand that any break in the skin may result in scarring. Scarring risk may be increased if I have a history of keloid scars, a history of hypertrophic scars, poor wound healing, infection, picking or scratching the area, poor aftercare, or smoking or vaping. I understand that scars may be temporary or permanent.

Pigmentation changes

I understand that pigmentation changes may occur after treatment. These may include darkening of the skin, lightening of the skin, post-inflammatory pigmentation, and persistent discolouration. The risk may be increased by sun exposure, sunbeds, darker skin types, inflammation, burns or poor aftercare. I understand I must protect the treated area from sun exposure and use SPF as advised.

Asymmetry

I understand that the human face and body are naturally asymmetrical. Endolaser may improve contour but may not correct all asymmetry. I understand that pre-existing asymmetry may remain, temporary swelling may make asymmetry look worse, final results may not be perfectly symmetrical, and additional treatment may be needed.

Dissatisfaction with results

I understand that dissatisfaction with aesthetic results is possible. This may include not enough tightening, not enough fat reduction, uneven result, result not matching expectation, need for further treatment, and difference between expected and actual outcome. I understand that no guarantee has been made regarding the final result.

I understand that this is not a complete list and that unforeseen risks may also occur. Some complications may require medication, medical review, referral, further treatment, corrective procedures or hospital care.

Aftercare

I understand that following aftercare instructions is essential to reduce risks and support healing. I understand that failure to follow aftercare may increase the risk of infection, prolonged swelling, bruising, delayed healing, pigmentation changes, poor results, scarring and complications. I agree to follow all aftercare instructions provided by Layali Clinic.

Aftercare day by day — immediately after the procedure

You may experience swelling, bruising, tenderness, redness, tightness and mild discomfort in the treated area. You may have small entry point marks where the fibre was inserted. You should:

  • Keep the area clean and dry.
  • Avoid touching the entry points unnecessarily.
  • Avoid applying makeup over entry points for at least 24–48 hours.
  • Avoid alcohol.
  • Avoid smoking or vaping where possible.
  • Avoid strenuous exercise.
  • Drink plenty of water.
  • Rest with your head elevated if the face or neck has been treated.

First 24–48 hours

Avoid: vigorous exercise, hot baths, saunas, steam rooms, sunbeds, excessive heat, alcohol, touching/rubbing/massaging the area, makeup over entry points, swimming, and applying active skincare products to the treated area. You may use a clean cool compress for short periods if advised by your practitioner. Do not apply ice directly to the skin.

Days 2–7

You may still experience swelling, bruising, tenderness and tightness. You should gently cleanse the skin, keep the treatment area clean, avoid aggressive massage unless advised, avoid active skincare ingredients, avoid retinol, acids and exfoliants, use SPF daily, continue to avoid saunas, steam rooms and sunbeds, drink plenty of water, eat protein-rich foods to support healing, and walk gently to support circulation.

Weeks 2–4

Swelling and bruising should gradually improve. You may notice firmness, tightness, mild lumps, unevenness, numbness and sensitivity — these can be part of the normal healing process. You should continue to protect the area from sun exposure, avoid harsh skincare if still sensitive, attend review appointments, and contact the clinic if symptoms worsen rather than improve.

Long-term care

To support your results: maintain a stable weight, stay hydrated, eat a balanced diet, prioritise protein intake, avoid smoking where possible, use SPF daily, avoid sunbeds, and follow maintenance advice from your practitioner.

Managing side effects at home

Swelling

Swelling is common and expected. To help manage swelling at home: sleep with your head elevated if face or neck has been treated, drink plenty of water, avoid alcohol for at least 48–72 hours, avoid high-salt foods, avoid intense exercise for the first week, use a clean cool compress for 10 minutes at a time if advised, do not apply ice directly to the skin, and do not over-ice the area. Swelling may be worse in the morning and improve during the day.

Bruising

Bruising can vary from mild to more noticeable. To help bruising settle: avoid alcohol, avoid strenuous exercise, avoid unnecessary pressure on the area, you may use arnica if suitable for you, avoid aspirin or ibuprofen unless prescribed by your doctor, keep hydrated, and allow time for bruising to fade naturally. Bruising may change colour as it heals.

Pain or tenderness

Mild pain or tenderness is common. To manage discomfort: take paracetamol if suitable for you, rest, avoid pressure on the treated area, avoid sleeping directly on the treated area, and avoid strenuous activity. Do not take ibuprofen, aspirin or anti-inflammatory medication unless prescribed or approved, as these may increase bruising and may interfere with the natural inflammatory healing response.

Tightness

Tightness is common after Endolaser and may be part of the collagen contraction and healing process. To manage tightness: stay hydrated, avoid over-manipulating the area, follow aftercare, and allow time for healing. Do not panic if the area feels tight, firm or slightly strange during the first few weeks.

Lumps, firmness or unevenness

Small areas of firmness, lumps or unevenness may occur during healing. To manage this: do not aggressively massage unless instructed, keep the area clean, monitor the area, attend your review appointment, and contact Layali Clinic if lumps become painful, red, hot or worsen. Some firmness can be part of normal collagen remodelling.

Numbness or tingling

Temporary numbness, tingling or altered sensation may occur. To manage this: avoid excessive pressure on the area, protect the area from heat or cold as sensation may be reduced, avoid using hot compresses, and monitor symptoms. Contact the clinic if numbness worsens, spreads or does not gradually improve.

Redness or heat

Mild redness and warmth can be normal shortly after treatment. However, contact Layali Clinic immediately if redness or heat gets worse, spreads, becomes painful, or is associated with fever, pus or discharge, or feeling unwell.

Entry point care

The entry points should be kept clean. You should avoid touching entry points with unwashed hands, avoid makeup over entry points for 24–48 hours, avoid swimming, hot tubs and saunas, avoid picking scabs, and allow the area to heal naturally.

Emergency warning signs — act quickly

Contact Layali Clinic immediately on 07787 708200 if you develop:

  • Severe or rapidly increasing pain
  • White, grey, blue, purple or black skin
  • Blistering or skin breakdown
  • Rapidly increasing swelling
  • Spreading redness or heat
  • Pus or discharge
  • Fever or feeling severely unwell

Call 999 or go to A&E immediately if you develop:

  • Breathing or swallowing difficulty
  • Facial or throat swelling
  • Chest pain
  • Visual disturbance or loss of vision
  • Weakness, confusion, collapse or other neurological symptoms

Acting quickly matters — some complications are time-critical, so please do not wait. Out of hours, if you cannot reach the clinic, call NHS 111, or 999/attend A&E for anything severe or rapidly worsening.

Tell any other healthcare professional (A&E, GP, 111) the treatment you had, the product, the date and the injection/treatment site(s), and show them this consent so they can help you quickly.
