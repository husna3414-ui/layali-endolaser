About the procedure

What is Endolaser?

Endolaser is a minimally invasive laser treatment used to tighten skin, stimulate collagen, improve skin laxity, contour the face or body, and reduce small areas of localised fat. A very fine sterile laser fibre is inserted under the skin through a small entry point, and laser energy is delivered beneath the skin to create controlled heating. This may contract existing collagen, stimulate new collagen, improve firmness and texture, tighten loose skin, improve jawline and neck definition, reduce jowls and double-chin fullness, and reduce small localised fat deposits. Endolaser is not surgery and does not produce the same result as a surgical facelift, neck lift or liposuction.

The procedure & sensations

The area is assessed, marked and cleansed. A local anaesthetic may be used. A small entry point is created and a fine sterile fibre inserted; laser energy is delivered to create controlled heating as the fibre is moved through the area. You may feel warmth, pressure, mild pulling, mild discomfort, tightness and a strange but tolerable sensation under the skin. Afterwards the area may feel swollen, tender, tight or bruised, and entry points may be covered with a small dressing. Procedure time varies by area.

Benefits, results & alternatives

Potential benefits

Skin tightening, improved facial contour and jawline definition, reduction in jowls, improved neck firmness, reduced double-chin fullness, improved skin quality, collagen stimulation, reduction of small localised fat deposits, improved confidence, and minimal downtime compared with surgery. Results vary between individuals and cannot be guaranteed.

Results & follow-up

Results develop gradually. Some tightening may be visible soon after treatment but can be affected by swelling — the final result is not immediate. Swelling may temporarily hide or exaggerate the result. Results usually develop over several weeks to months; collagen production may continue for 3–6 months and some clients improve for up to 12 months. One session may not achieve your desired result and additional or maintenance treatment may be recommended. Ageing continues naturally, and lifestyle factors (smoking, alcohol, diet, hydration, weight changes, sun exposure) may affect the result.

Alternative treatments

Alternatives may include: no treatment, surgical facelift, surgical neck lift, liposuction, radiofrequency skin tightening, HIFU, RF microneedling, thread lift, dermal fillers, fat dissolving injections, skin boosters, weight loss, or other practitioner-recommended treatments. You have the right to decline treatment or choose an alternative.

Risks & complications

No procedure is entirely risk-free. Endolaser is minimally invasive and involves inserting a laser fibre beneath the skin, so there are inherent and potential risks. Unforeseen risks not listed here may also occur. Some complications may require medication, medical review, referral, further treatment, corrective procedures or hospital care.

Common (usually temporary)

  • Swelling, bruising, redness
  • Tenderness, tightness, mild discomfort
  • Warmth in the area
  • Small entry-point marks & minor bleeding
  • Temporary unevenness or asymmetry
  • Temporary firmness / mild lumps
  • Skin sensitivity, temporary numbness
  • Feeling faint

Uncommon

  • Prolonged swelling / bruising / tenderness
  • Persistent firmness, fibrosis, nodules
  • Irregular contour, dents, uneven fat reduction
  • Delayed healing, infection
  • Pigmentation changes (hyper/hypo)
  • Prolonged numbness, nerve irritation
  • Unsatisfactory outcome / further treatment

Rare but serious

  • Burns, thermal injury, blistering, skin necrosis
  • Permanent scarring / keloid / pigmentation change
  • Permanent numbness, altered sensation, nerve damage
  • Temporary or permanent weakness, chronic pain
  • Fat necrosis, infection requiring antibiotics, abscess
  • Damage to underlying structures, significant asymmetry
  • Permanent contour irregularity, need for surgical correction, hospital treatment
Damage to underlying structures

Endolaser is performed beneath the skin; although care is taken, there is a risk of trauma or irritation to underlying structures including blood vessels, nerves, lymphatic vessels, fat, connective tissue, salivary glands (in facial areas), muscle or fascia and other soft tissue. In rare cases injury may cause persistent problems with appearance, sensation or function.

Infection

Any procedure involving entry through the skin carries an infection risk. Signs include increasing redness, increasing swelling, heat, worsening pain, pus or discharge, fever, feeling unwell, or red streaking. If you experience any of these you must contact Layali Clinic immediately and may need antibiotics or medical review.

Burns & thermal injury

Endolaser works by delivering heat beneath the skin. Despite careful technique, excessive heat may cause burns, blistering, skin damage, necrosis, pigmentation change, scarring or delayed healing. These may require medical treatment and may leave permanent marks.

Numbness, nerve irritation & altered sensation

Temporary numbness, tingling or altered sensation can occur from swelling, inflammation or irritation of small sensory nerves, and usually improves with time. Rarely, numbness, altered sensation, hypersensitivity, weakness or nerve symptoms may be prolonged or permanent.

Fibrosis, lumps & unevenness

During healing you may feel firm areas, lumps, nodules, rope-like firmness, uneven texture or tight bands, caused by swelling, inflammation, collagen remodelling, fibrosis or healing. Most mild irregularities improve over time; some may persist and require massage, review, further or corrective treatment. Do not aggressively massage unless advised.

Fat reduction & contour irregularity

Where used for fat reduction, the reduction may be uneven. Risks include uneven fat reduction, indentations, depressions, asymmetry, over-reduction, under-correction, irregular contour, remaining loose skin and need for additional treatment. Endolaser is not a weight-loss treatment.

Scarring

Although entry points are small, any break in the skin may scar. Risk is increased by a history of keloid/hypertrophic scars, poor wound healing, infection, picking/scratching, poor aftercare, or smoking/vaping. Scars may be temporary or permanent.

Pigmentation changes

Pigmentation changes may occur — darkening, lightening, post-inflammatory pigmentation or persistent discolouration — with risk increased by sun exposure, sunbeds, darker skin types, inflammation, burns or poor aftercare. Protect the area from sun and use SPF as advised.

Asymmetry & dissatisfaction

The face and body are naturally asymmetrical; Endolaser may improve but not fully correct asymmetry, and swelling can temporarily worsen it. Dissatisfaction is possible (not enough tightening/fat reduction, uneven result, result not matching expectation, need for further treatment). No guarantee has been made regarding the final result.

Aftercare (please read)

Following aftercare is essential to reduce risks and support healing. Failure to follow aftercare may increase the risk of infection, prolonged swelling, bruising, delayed healing, pigmentation changes, poor results, scarring and complications.
Immediately after & first 24–48 hours

You may have swelling, bruising, tenderness, redness, tightness and mild discomfort, and small entry-point marks. Keep the area clean and dry; avoid touching entry points; no makeup over entry points for 24–48 hours; avoid alcohol, smoking/vaping and strenuous exercise; drink plenty of water; rest with your head elevated if face/neck treated. In the first 24–48 hours also avoid hot baths, saunas, steam rooms, sunbeds, excessive heat, swimming, rubbing/massaging the area, and active skincare. A clean cool compress may be used for short periods if advised — do not apply ice directly to the skin.

Days 2–7

You may still have swelling, bruising, tenderness and tightness. Gently cleanse and keep the area clean; avoid aggressive massage, active ingredients, retinol, acids and exfoliants; use SPF daily; continue to avoid saunas, steam rooms and sunbeds; hydrate well; eat protein-rich foods; walk gently to support circulation.

Weeks 2–4 & long-term

Swelling and bruising should gradually improve. You may notice firmness, tightness, mild lumps, unevenness, numbness or sensitivity as part of normal healing. Continue to protect from sun, avoid harsh skincare if sensitive, attend reviews, and contact the clinic if symptoms worsen rather than improve. Long-term: maintain a stable weight, stay hydrated, eat a balanced protein-rich diet, avoid smoking, use SPF daily, avoid sunbeds, and follow your practitioner's maintenance advice.

Managing side effects at home

Swelling: elevate head, hydrate, avoid alcohol 48–72h, avoid salt and intense exercise for the first week, cool compress 10 min if advised (never ice directly). Bruising: avoid alcohol, strenuous exercise and pressure; arnica if suitable; avoid aspirin/ibuprofen unless prescribed. Pain: paracetamol if suitable; rest; avoid pressure and sleeping on the area; do not take ibuprofen/aspirin/anti-inflammatories unless approved. Tightness: normal — hydrate, avoid over-manipulating, allow healing. Lumps/firmness: do not aggressively massage unless instructed; monitor; contact us if painful, red, hot or worsening. Numbness: avoid pressure and extreme heat/cold; monitor; contact us if it worsens or spreads. Entry points: keep clean, avoid makeup 24–48h, avoid swimming/hot tubs/saunas, don't pick scabs.

Contact Layali Clinic urgently if you experience

Increasing or severe pain · worsening or sudden swelling · increasing/spreading redness or heat · pus or discharge · fever · feeling unwell · blistering · skin turning white, grey, purple or black · severe asymmetry · loss of movement · worsening numbness · any symptom that concerns you. If seriously unwell, seek urgent medical care or call emergency services.
