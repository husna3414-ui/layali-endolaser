After Your Treatment

Endolaser Aftercare & Recovery

A calm, complete guide to your recovery — what's normal, what's not, and how your result unfolds over the months ahead.

Your Endolaser treatment works by delivering laser energy beneath the skin to stimulate collagen, tighten tissue and, in some cases, reduce unwanted fat. A little improvement may be visible immediately, but the majority of your result develops gradually over the following 3–6 months as your body produces new collagen.

Every person's healing journey is different. The first few days are not your final result — trust the process, and never hesitate to reach out to us.

What is completely normal?

During the first few days and weeks, it is completely normal to experience:

Normal & expected

  • Swelling, bruising & redness
  • Tightness & tenderness
  • Mild numbness & tingling
  • Areas of firmness
  • Temporary unevenness
  • Mild pulling sensations
  • Skin feeling "strange"
  • Changes from one day to the next
Healing is not linear. Some days you may feel tighter, while on other days swelling may temporarily make the area look fuller. This does not mean anything is wrong.

What is NOT normal — contact us immediately

Please contact Layali Clinic straight away if you experience:
  • Increasing pain rather than gradual improvement
  • Redness that keeps spreading
  • Significant heat in the area
  • Fever
  • Pus or discharge
  • Skin blistering
  • Skin becoming white, grey, purple or black
  • Rapidly worsening swelling
  • Difficulty swallowing or breathing
  • Any symptom causing you concern

Please don't judge your results too early

One of the most common concerns after Endolaser is "I don't see a difference" or "it looked better yesterday." This is almost always because swelling changes throughout healing.

Your final result is not visible during the first few days or weeks. The majority of improvement develops gradually through collagen stimulation over the following months. Patience is essential — and very much rewarded.

Understanding firmness & fibrosis

Many clients notice firm or rope-like areas beneath the skin during healing. This is often caused by swelling, inflammation, collagen remodelling or early fibrosis.

In most cases it improves naturally as healing progresses, though these areas can remain noticeable for several weeks before softening. Do not aggressively massage unless specifically advised by your practitioner — and contact us if an area becomes painful, red, hot or worsens.

Managing common side effects

Swelling

Often more noticeable in the morning, improving through the day.

To reduce swelling

  • Sleep with your head elevated on 2–3 pillows
  • Stay well hydrated
  • Walk regularly
  • Avoid excessive salt & alcohol
  • Cool compress, 10 minutes at a time
  • No strenuous exercise for 7 days

Important

  • Do NOT apply ice directly onto the skin
  • Excessive icing can delay healing

Bruising

To help it settle

  • Arnica gel on intact skin / arnica tablets
  • Bromelain supplements may help
  • Gentle walking improves circulation
  • Stay hydrated

Bruising typically resolves within 7–21 days and may change colour as it heals.

Tenderness

You may

  • Take Paracetamol if required
  • Rest
  • Avoid pressure on the area

Avoid (unless prescribed)

  • Aspirin
  • Ibuprofen
  • Anti-inflammatories

Inflammation is part of the collagen-building process — anti-inflammatories may interfere with this response.

Tightness

A pulling or tightening sensation is a positive sign and often indicates collagen contraction. Stay hydrated, follow your aftercare and allow time — it may continue for several weeks.

Numbness or altered sensation

Temporary numbness can occur from localised swelling around small sensory nerves, and usually improves over several weeks. Contact us if it worsens rather than improves.

Gentle movement & lymphatic drainage

Short walks are encouraged during recovery. Gentle movement supports circulation, lymphatic drainage, swelling reduction and healing.

Movement guide

  • First 48 hours: avoid the gym, running, weightlifting & high-intensity exercise
  • Days 3–7: light walking is encouraged
  • After 7 days: most clients gradually return to normal exercise
  • Avoid prolonged bed rest unless advised otherwise

Protect your result while sleeping

For the first 7–14 days

  • Sleep on your back
  • Elevate your head & use extra pillows
  • Avoid sleeping face-down
  • Avoid pressing the treated area into the pillow

Reducing pressure helps minimise swelling and discomfort, and protects your developing result.

Hydration & nutrition

Your body needs nutrients to build collagen. Aim for 2–3 litres of water daily, increase protein, and eat plenty of fresh fruit and vegetables.

Vitamin C rich

  • Oranges
  • Strawberries
  • Kiwi
  • Peppers
  • Broccoli

Protein rich

  • Chicken
  • Fish
  • Eggs
  • Greek yoghurt
  • Tofu & lean meats

Smoking, vaping & alcohol

Smoking & vaping

  • Avoid for at least 2 weeks (longer is better)
  • Avoid vaping where possible
  • Smoking slows healing & reduces collagen

Alcohol

  • Avoid for at least 48–72 hours
  • Increases swelling & bruising
  • Delays healing

Skincare & sun protection

First 48 hours — use only

  • Gentle cleanser
  • Simple moisturiser
  • SPF 50

Avoid for now

  • Retinol & tretinoin
  • Exfoliating acids
  • Strong actives
  • Scrubs

These can be reintroduced gradually once healed.

Sun protection — daily SPF 50 is essential

  • No sunbeds
  • No excessive sun exposure
  • No prolonged direct sunlight — for at least 4 weeks
