What is Endolaser?

Endolaser, also known as endolifting or intradermal laser, is a minimally invasive laser treatment. A very fine optical fibre is gently introduced beneath the skin through a tiny entry point, and laser energy is delivered under the skin to create controlled heating in the deeper tissue layers.

This heat helps to tighten existing collagen fibres, stimulate new collagen production and, in selected areas, reduce small pockets of fat. Because the energy is delivered beneath the skin rather than only from the surface, Endolaser can target the support structures more directly than many surface-based treatments.

How does Endolaser work?

Endolaser works in three main ways:

1. Immediate tissue tightening

The controlled heat causes existing collagen fibres to contract, creating an early firming and tightening effect.

2. New collagen production

The treatment stimulates your body's natural healing response, encouraging fresh collagen and elastin over the following weeks and months — which is why results continue to improve gradually after treatment.

3. Subtle fat reduction

In areas such as the double chin or lower face, Endolaser can help reduce small, stubborn fat pockets for a more sculpted, defined contour.

Important: Endolaser is not a weight-loss treatment and is not suitable for large-volume fat removal. It is also not a substitute for a surgical facelift.

Who is Endolaser best for?

May suit you if you have

  • Mild to moderate skin laxity
  • Early jowls
  • A soft or blurred jawline
  • Fullness under the chin
  • Loose or crepey neck skin
  • Mild facial sagging
  • Small stubborn fat pockets
  • Good general health & realistic expectations

May not be suitable if you

  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Have an active infection or open wound in the area
  • Have uncontrolled diabetes
  • Have a bleeding/clotting disorder or take blood thinners
  • Have poor wound healing or keloid scarring history
  • Have certain autoimmune conditions
  • Have very advanced laxity better suited to surgery
  • Have unrealistic expectations

It is especially suitable for clients who feel they need more than HIFU but are not ready for surgery. Suitability is confirmed during consultation, and treatment may be refused if it is not safe or appropriate.

What happens during treatment?

1. Consultation

We assess your skin, laxity, facial structure, medical history and goals. Clinical photographs are taken for your records.

2. Preparation

The area is cleansed, marked and prepared. Local anaesthetic is used to keep you comfortable.

3. Treatment

The fine fibre is inserted through tiny entry points and moved carefully beneath the skin while laser energy is delivered. Most clients feel pressure, warmth or mild pulling rather than pain.

4. After treatment

You can usually go home shortly after. There are no stitches and no hospital stay.

What does it feel like?

Most clients tolerate Endolaser very well. You may feel warmth, pressure, mild pulling, tightness, slight discomfort or a strange sensation under the skin. Local anaesthetic helps keep the procedure comfortable.

Downtime & recovery

After treatment it is normal to experience swelling, bruising, redness, tenderness, tightness, temporary numbness, mild firmness and small entry-point marks.

Most clients return to normal daily activities within a few days, although visible swelling or bruising can take 1–2 weeks to settle. Healing varies from person to person.

Results timeline

Immediately

You may notice early tightening, though swelling can temporarily affect the appearance.

2–4 weeks

3 months

6 months

When many clients see their most visible improvement.

Up to 12 months

Collagen remodelling may continue, especially in skin quality and firmness.

How many sessions will I need?

Many clients achieve a visible improvement from one session. Some may require a second depending on skin laxity, age, treatment area, amount of fat present, collagen response and desired result. We will always assess honestly and recommend only what is appropriate.

How long do results last?

Results can last around 1–3 years depending on your skin, age, lifestyle and natural ageing. Maintenance may be recommended every 12–24 months. To help maintain results: use SPF daily, avoid smoking, stay hydrated, maintain a stable weight, follow good skincare and protect your skin from sun damage.

Risks & side effects

Endolaser is generally well tolerated, but it is still a minimally invasive procedure and all treatments carry risk.

Common side effects

  • Swelling
  • Bruising
  • Tenderness
  • Redness
  • Tightness
  • Temporary numbness
  • Mild firmness
  • Temporary unevenness

Less common or rare

  • Infection
  • Burns / blistering
  • Pigment changes
  • Nerve irritation
  • Prolonged numbness
  • Small lumps or nodules
  • Fibrosis / asymmetry
  • Contour irregularity / scarring
  • Unsatisfactory result / further treatment

All risks are explained fully before treatment, and you will complete a medical history and consent form.

Frequently asked questions

Is Endolaser painful?

Most clients find it very manageable. Local anaesthetic is used to keep you comfortable — you may feel warmth, pressure or mild pulling.

When will I see results?

Some tightening may be visible early, but the best results develop gradually over 3–6 months as collagen production increases.

How much downtime is there?

Most clients return to normal activities within a few days. Swelling and bruising may take 1–2 weeks to settle.

Is it safe?

Endolaser is considered safe when performed by a trained practitioner, but it is still a minimally invasive treatment and carries risks, which are explained before treatment.

Will it look natural?

Yes — the aim is a natural-looking lift and contour. The treatment works with your own collagen rather than changing your features dramatically.

Is it a facelift alternative?

It can be a good non-surgical option for mild to moderate laxity, but it will not achieve the same result as surgery in cases of severe sagging.

Will I need more than one session?

Many clients only need one, but some may need a second depending on skin laxity, fat volume and desired result.

Are there scars?

The fibre is inserted through tiny entry points that usually heal very discreetly, but any break in the skin carries a small risk of marking or scarring.
